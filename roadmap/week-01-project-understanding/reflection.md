# Week 01 Reflection: Understanding the Product Before Coding

## Purpose

Reflection helps you turn planning into learning. Save your answers in:

```text
docs/evidence/week-01/reflection-answers.md
```

Write in complete sentences. Do not copy from the roadmap. Use your own words.

---

## Section 1: Product Understanding

### Prompt 1: Product Idea

Explain LeafGuard AI in your own words. Who is it for, and what problem does it help with?

### Prompt 2: Honest Scope

What can the final app reasonably claim to do? What should it not claim to do?

### Prompt 3: Why Mobile?

Why does this project make sense as an Android app instead of only a website, notebook, or backend script?

---

## Section 2: User Journey

### Prompt 4: Main Flow

Write the main user journey from opening the app to seeing a result.

### Prompt 5: Most Important Step

Which step in the journey feels most important to you, and why?

### Prompt 6: Beginner Confusion

Which part of the journey is still unclear? What question do you need answered in a future week?

---

## Section 3: Weekly Growth

### Prompt 7: Week 01 Contribution

What does Week 01 contribute to the final product, even though no app code is written yet?

### Prompt 8: Week 02 Readiness

What do you expect to build in Week 02? What do you need to learn first?

### Prompt 9: Validation Mindset

Why should each week validate only the slice built that week?

---

## Section 4: Evidence and Learning Discipline

### Prompt 10: Best Evidence

Which Week 01 artifact best proves your understanding: product idea, user journey, screen map, system sketch, or growth map? Explain why.

### Prompt 11: Avoiding Copy-Paste Learning

How will you make sure you are learning the product step by step instead of copying from the finished code?

### Prompt 12: Personal Gap List

List three topics that still feel unfamiliar. For each, write which future week will teach it.

Use this format:

| Topic I Do Not Fully Understand Yet | Future Week | What I Hope To Learn |
|---|---:|---|

---

## Completion Check

Before finishing reflection, answer yes or no:

- [ ] I can explain the base product idea.
- [ ] I can explain the main user journey.
- [ ] I understand why Week 01 does not build Android code yet.
- [ ] I know what evidence I saved.
- [ ] I know what Week 02 should build next.

If any answer is no, return to `learning-notes.md` and revise your Week 01 artifacts.
