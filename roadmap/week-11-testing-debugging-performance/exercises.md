# Week 11 Exercises: Testing and Debugging

Save six files under `docs/evidence/week-11/exercises/`.

1. `exercise-01-test-pyramid.md`: map 12 product risks to unit/instrumentation/Python/manual layers and justify cheapest layer.
2. `exercise-02-arrange-act-assert.md`: design tests for share rounding/privacy, eight-field JSON, and 10-entry XML.
3. `exercise-03-room-tests.md`: plan isolated CRUD/order/detail/delete and v1->v2 migration with preserved null location.
4. `exercise-04-ui-tests.md`: define Result/Settings visibility assertions and what Espresso does not prove.
5. `exercise-05-performance-regression.md`: distinguish 15-second smoke budget from benchmark; plan repeated device measurements.
6. `exercise-06-debug-record.md`: record red test, stack trace, falsifiable hypothesis, discriminating check, fix, focused/full green evidence.

Completion requires choosing layers correctly, explaining schema snapshots, distinguishing compile versus execute, and stating residual manual risks.
